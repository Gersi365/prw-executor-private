PURPOSE

Phase 128 establishes a replay-resistant enrolled-device session-authentication foundation. The phase proves possession of the already-enrolled device private identity for one fresh server-issued session challenge and produces an immutable authenticated session identity.

Authentication and authorization remain separate. Successful Phase 128 authentication does not grant file, terminal, forwarding, networking, DNS, administrative, or workspace-management capabilities.
