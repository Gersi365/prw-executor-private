ORDERING SEMANTICS

The guard is outside `run_authenticated_session_worker(...)`.

Normal return:

1. the worker body returns;
2. worker-body-owned state, including `LocalLinuxWorkerPermit`, is released;
3. wrapper guard drops and emits the completion wake;
4. caller may drain wake and observe released capacity.

Panic/unwind:

1. panic originates inside the worker body;
2. callee-owned state unwinds, releasing the capacity permit;
3. wrapper-frame completion guard then drops during unwind and emits the wake;
4. existing join semantics still classify the worker panic independently.

The eventfd counter carries no worker-result semantics.
